## Summary

In `app/Atlas/Core/Session.swift:111-119`, `Session.normalize(_ address: String)` prepends `http://` only when `!text.contains("://")`, and does not verify that `components.scheme` is `"http"` or `"https"`. Any scheme containing `://` with a non-empty host (for example `ftp://...` or `file://host/...`) passes `normalize()` and `Session.connectLink(_:)`. Separately, `app/Atlas/Core/API.swift:55-66` force-unwraps `URLComponents(url: ..., resolvingAgainstBaseURL: false)!` and `components.url!`.

## Evidence

- `app/Atlas/Core/Session.swift:114-118`: checks `components.host` is non-empty, but never checks `components.scheme == "http" || components.scheme == "https"`.
- `app/Atlas/Core/API.swift:56, 58, 63, 65`: four force-unwraps (`!`) on `URLComponents` and `components.url`.

## Risk

Low: `connectLink` requires explicit owner confirmation before connecting (`Session.swift:96-108`), and `connect()` validates `info.name == "atlas"` over HTTP. Still, restricting `Session.normalize` to `http`/`https` and avoiding force-unwraps in `API.swift` prevents unexpected scheme handling or crashes on malformed inputs.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `410e5de`. Restricted `Session.normalize` in `app/Atlas/Core/Session.swift` strictly to `http`/`https` schemes with a non-empty host, and replaced `URLComponents` force-unwraps in `app/Atlas/Core/API.swift` with safe `guard let` throws (`APIError.invalidURL`).

## How to verify

- Verify `Session.normalize("file://example.com")` and `Session.normalize("javascript://example.com")` return `nil`, while `http://` and `https://` addresses continue to normalize cleanly.
