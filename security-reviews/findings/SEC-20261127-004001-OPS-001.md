## Summary

Three operational hardening gaps affect host secret and backup confidentiality:
1. `scripts/pg-backup/backup.sh:23-39` creates `$BACKUP_DIR` and writes `atlas_*.dump` and `globals_*.sql` (containing database roles, password hashes, `api_keys` rows, and user metadata) with the process's default umask (typically `022`, making dumps world-readable).
2. `scripts/atlas/install.sh:9-12` checks that `/etc/atlas/atlas.env` exists, but does not verify or enforce mode `0600` (or `0640` owned by root/atlas) on it.
3. `scripts/atlas/atlas-server.service:7-20` sets no `UMask=0077` or baseline systemd hardening (`PrivateTmp=yes`, `ProtectKernelTunables=yes`, `ProtectKernelModules=yes`, `ProtectControlGroups=yes`), despite holding `/etc/atlas/atlas.env` secrets and running in the `docker` group.

## Evidence

- `scripts/pg-backup/backup.sh:23-38`: no `umask 077` or `chmod 700 "$BACKUP_DIR"` / `chmod 600` on `$dump.part` and `$globals.part`.
- `scripts/atlas/install.sh:9-12`: checks `sudo test -f /etc/atlas/atlas.env` only, without verifying permissions.
- `scripts/atlas/atlas-server.service:7-20`: contains only `Type`, `User`, `Environment`, `EnvironmentFile`, `ExecStart`, `Restart`, `RestartSec`.

## Risk

Any other local account or backup-reading process can read Postgres dumps or an accidentally world-readable `/etc/atlas/atlas.env`, and `atlas-server` runs with broader host surface than required.

## Fix and current state

Open. Add `umask 077` and `chmod 700 "$BACKUP_DIR"` at the top of `scripts/pg-backup/backup.sh`, enforce/warn on non-`0600` permissions of `/etc/atlas/atlas.env` in `scripts/atlas/install.sh`, and add `UMask=0077` plus compatible systemd hardening directives (`PrivateTmp=yes`, `ProtectKernelModules=yes`, `ProtectControlGroups=yes`) to `scripts/atlas/atlas-server.service` (keeping compatibility with `sudo -n systemctl poweroff|reboot` and PTY terminal).

## How to verify
